## Required review before distribution

Before fieldwork, review each form for the complete segment-specific questions in the repository interview guides, choose whether names and email addresses will be collected, configure the response destination, publish the forms, and test each response path. Do not distribute the forms until the final questions and privacy settings have been approved.

The detailed question specifications remain in `draft_google_form_specs.csv` and the segment-specific interview guides in the repository. These live forms are initial draft shells created to establish the Google Forms assets; they should not yet be treated as the complete validated instruments.

## Privacy and consent

Do not request child names, student identifiers, unpublished manuscripts, confidential contracts, assessment records, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Participation must remain voluntary, and no form should be distributed to a person without an appropriate research-contact basis or consent.

## Publication status

The forms are intentionally left unpublished pending approval. Publishing or distributing them is a separate action and should occur only after the user approves the final content, settings, and recruitment list.
